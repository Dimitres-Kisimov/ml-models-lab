This lab re-implements the cores of well-known published methods for teaching /
demonstration on synthetic data. The methods and their authors:

## Methods and papers

- **DeepAR** - Salinas, Flunkert, Gasthaus & Januschowski, *International Journal
  of Forecasting* 2020. https://arxiv.org/abs/1704.04110
- **N-BEATS** - Oreshkin, Carpov, Chapados & Bengio, ICLR 2020.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.10437
- **Croston's method** - Croston, *Operational Research Quarterly* 1972; ADI/CV2
  classification - Syntetos & Boylan, 2005.
- **fastText** - Joulin, Grave, Bojanowski & Mikolov, 2016.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1607.01759
- **TextCNN** - Kim, EMNLP 2014. https://arxiv.org/abs/1408.5882
- **Character-level ConvNets** - Zhang, Zhao & LeCun, NIPS 2015.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1509.01626
- **Feature hashing** - Weinberger, Dasgupta, Langford, Smola & Attenberg, ICML
  2009. https://arxiv.org/abs/0902.2206
- **Autoencoder anomaly detection** - Sakurada & Yairi, MLSDA 2014.
- **Replicator neural networks for outliers** - Hawkins, He, Williams & Baxter,
  2002.
- **Deep SVDD** - Ruff et al., ICML 2018.
- **RFM optimal selection** - Bult & Wansbeek, *Marketing Science* 1995.
- **Focal loss** - Lin, Goyal, Girshick, He & Dollar, ICCV 2017.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1708.02002
- **Platt scaling** - Platt, 1999.
- **Calibration / ECE** - Guo, Pleiss, Sun & Weinberger, ICML 2017.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.04599
- **Ridge regression** - Hoerl & Kennard, *Technometrics* 1970.
- **Lasso** - Tibshirani, *JRSS-B* 1996; coordinate descent - Friedman, Hastie &
  Tibshirani, 2010.
- **Lerner index** - Lerner, *Review of Economic Studies* 1934.

## Software

- PyTorch, NumPy, SciPy, pandas, matplotlib.

All datasets used here are **synthetic and seeded** (see `mllab/synth.py`). No
